### 9. `[S]` Downloaded vs. Self-made

| | Downloaded | Self-made |
|---|---|---|
| Fits my lesson | roughly | exactly |
| Terminology & level | someone else's | my students' |
| Language | often only one | as many as I need |
| Examples | generic | local, current, ours |
| Time to find | can be hours | minutes, plus checking |

*Delivery note:* Hlavní argument části 2. Bilingvní výuka je silný příklad — materiál v obou jazycích se stejnou terminologií.

### 12. `[S]` How I make it

1. The key is to know what I want and to be able to describe it.
2. Then I just ask the AI agent to do it – it's important to provide materials and context (age group, the subject matter, ...)
3. Adjust (ask for changes) a few times to fit my needs.
