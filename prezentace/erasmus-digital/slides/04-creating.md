### 22. `[S]` Why making works

- every medium **forces decisions**: what to show, what to leave out, in what order
- decisions reveal understanding — or its absence
- the product is **evidence of thinking**, not just of effort

### 24. `[S]` Case study: prompting a character

*Layout:* two

#### The task

- students analyse a fictional character: personality, relationships, communication
- then they **write the instructions for a chatbot** that plays the character
- classmates interview the bot

#### Why it works

- you cannot prompt what you have not understood
- the analysis becomes **testable**: does the bot behave like the character?
